How it stays fair

Runs many elections at once

Every department can hold its election on its own schedule, in parallel, without interfering with the others.

Anonymous by design

A ballot is stored on its own, with nothing linking it back to who cast it — not even to the admin.

No impersonation

Only people the admin has pre-registered can log in, using a Voter ID and a password only they receive.

One vote per person

The moment a ballot is cast, that Voter ID is locked out of voting again in the same election.
